About Bacterial Spot

Bacterial spot is caused by four species of Xanthomonas (X. vesicatoria, X. euvesicatoria, X. gardneri, and X. perforans). It penetrates through stomata and plant wounds during high canopy humidity, producing foliar necrosis, leaf drop, and sunken lesions on fruit that reduce yield quality.

Bacterial Pathology High Humidity Vector Foliar & Fruit Tissue

Diagnostic Markers & Etiology

Common Symptoms

  • ✓ Small angular water-soaked lesions turning dark brown or black
  • ✓ Yellow chlorotic halos surrounding irregular leaf spots
  • ✓ Raised blister-like scabs on stems and developing fruits

Possible Causes

  • ✓ Warm driving rain and splash dispersal from infected foliage
  • ✓ Bacterial survival in soil crop residue and solanaceous weeds
  • ✓ Prolonged leaf wetness exceeding 6–8 hours

Irrigation Advisory

Primary Protocol: Root-Zone Drip Watering — Avoid overhead sprinkler spray to keep foliage dry and suppress bacterial splash transmission.

✓ Avoid overhead irrigation entirely during active disease phase
✓ Use drip line deliver directly at the root zone in early morning
✓ Check tensiometer or soil moisture before irrigating
✓ Resume standard watering cycle once ambient humidity decreases
