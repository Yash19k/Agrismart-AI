## Brand & Style

This design system expresses an editorial, high-trust synthesis of deep agrarian heritage and cutting-edge ecological intelligence. Built for modern agricultural enterprises, ag-tech AI platforms, and sustainable food systems, it rejects the sterile, tech-heavy aesthetics of typical SaaS in favor of an organic, prestige-editorial atmosphere.

The visual style blends **Editorial Elegance** with **Organic Tactility**:
- **Serene Atmosphere:** A luminous canvas of pale mint-tinted warm whites, layered over soft atmospheric backdrops that recall misty morning fields and botanical groves.
- **Literary Prestige:** Graceful, high-contrast serif typography delivers timeless authority, balanced by calm, neutral geometric grotesques for operational data and microcopy.
- **Harmonious Accents:** Forest greens, muted sage, and delicate herbal tints work harmoniously to signal renewal, precision yield, and sustainable stewardship without visual noise.
- **Restrained Depth:** Floating sheets, gentle pill-shaped controls, and hairline translucent strokes build a clean, tranquil hierarchy suitable for long-form reading, executive summaries, and farmer-facing analytics.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the lifecycle of cultivated earth, morning light, and canopy shade. 

- **Primary (`#1B4D3E`):** A deep, commanding forest green that anchors editorial headlines, key branding marks, and high-emphasis primary button surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#407B57`):** A vibrant foliage green used for dual-tone heading accents, active status badges, and interactive feedback states.
- **Tertiary (`#8FA382`):** Muted sage tone reserved for secondary button outlines, delicate borders, pill chip surfaces, and muted agricultural iconography.
- **Neutral (`#24332C`):** Deep botanical charcoal used for high-legibility body prose, stat labels, and secondary interface chrome, avoiding pure synthetic blacks.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base:** `#F4F8F4` to `#F9FBF8`, a warm, mint-washed milk white that prevents screen fatigue.
- **Card Surface:** `#FFFFFF` pure white floating tiles framed with delicate translucent borders (`rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.08)`).
- **Subtle Surface Container:** `#EEF5EF`, used for tab panels, interactive toggles, and metadata chips.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on a pairing of classical literary forms with clean mid-century functionalism:

- **Editorial Headings (`EB Garamond`):** Headlines use this humanist serif to evoke agrarian almanacs, architectural monographs, and academic botany journals. Multi-word headlines leverage dual-tone coloration, where key terms shift into vibrant foliage green (`#407B57`) against deep forest tones.
- **Body Text (`DM Sans`):** Smooth, low-contrast, highly legible grotesk proportions maintain reading comfort across long narrative blocks and tactical case studies.
- **Interface & Metadata (`Inter`):** Selected for labels, system pills, metrics captions, and navigation bar links to guarantee crisp legibility at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a central, framed page canvas placed against an environmental, photographic background depicting lush rolling farmland or botanical textures.

### Layout Model
- **Central Floating Stage:** The primary web experience occupies a centered column with a maximum width of `1240px`, inset within the viewport to let the peripheral nature imagery breathe.
- **12-Column Grid:** Desktop layouts use a 12-column structure with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and `2rem` (`32px`) internal padding.
- **Dual-Panel Compositions:** Feature blocks and solutions pair an interactive side tab bar (1/3 column) with an illustrative media/preview card (2/3 column).
- **Metric Row Rhythm:** High-impact metric callouts feature liberal vertical whitespace (`space-xl`), creating breathing room between narrative arcs.

### Responsiveness
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Outer margin collapses to `1rem`. Dual panels stack vertically. Display titles shift to mobile-specific scale tokens.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with 2-column feature card distributions.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column grid with generous section buffers (`4rem` to `6rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through delicate atmospheric layering rather than harsh dropshadows.

- **Atmospheric Floor:** The lowest visual tier consists of ambient background imagery gently veiled by a mint-to-cream gradient overlay (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(244,248,244,0.85) 0%, #F4F8F4 100%)`).
- **Floating Stage:** The central content wrapper floats atop the canvas using an ultra-wide, soft diffuse shadow: `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.06)`.
- **Card Tier:** Interactive service and review cards feature pure white surfaces with an ultra-thin perimeter stroke (`1px solid rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.07)`) and subtle ground shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.04)`.
- **Hover Micro-Lift:** On interaction, cards smoothly translate `-2px` on the Y-axis, transitioning shadow to `0 16px 32px -6px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a hybrid approach that reconciles organic curves with structured geometry:

- **Buttons & Interactive Tags:** Full pill radius (`9999px`) for all actionable controls, including primary CTA buttons, consultation buttons, and filter chips.
- **Containers & Surface Cards:** `rounded-lg` (`16px`) to `rounded-xl` (`24px`) for primary cards, tab panels, and image frames, balancing architectural structure with soft tactile comfort.
- **Hero Framing Curves:** Section dividers and scenic framing masks employ gentle convex arc geometries, echoing undulating agricultural hillsides.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** Background filled with `#1B4D3E`, label in pure white (`DM Sans`, medium), `rounded-full`, vertical padding `12px`, horizontal padding `28px`. Hover state lightens to `#266350`.
- **Secondary / Outlined Pill:** Transparent background with a `1px` or `1.5px` border in `#8FA382` or `#1B4D3E`, label in `#1B4D3E`. Hover fills with subtle mint wash `rgba(64, 123, 87, 0.06)`.
- **Icon Action Buttons:** Circular controls (`40px x 40px`) with a fine border, containing directional arrows for slider or pagination patterns.

### Cards
- **Feature & Service Cards:** Constructed on `#FFFFFF` backgrounds with `16px` to `20px` corner radii. Padding is consistent at `24px` (`space-lg`). Includes a mono-line botanical/agronomy icon seated in a pale sage container or directly tint-colored in `#407B57`.
- **Review & Testimonial Cards:** White surface with quote marks set in muted forest green, body quote text set in `DM Sans` 15px, and metadata separated by light horizontal rules.

### Tabbed Master-Detail Panels
- **Vertical Tab List:** Left rail holding clean, single-line text tabs with soft `8px` radii.
- **Active Tab:** Pale sage highlight background (`#D8E8D9`), dark green bold text, and a crisp subtle left border accent.
- **Content Area:** High-resolution preview window on the right with a `16px` radius, showcasing ag-tech equipment, robotics, or sensor data overlays.

### Metric Callouts
- Large numbers rendered in `EB Garamond` (`stat-display`, 48px) in `#1B4D3E` or `#24332C`.
- Descriptive subtitle placed immediately below in `DM Sans` (`body-sm`), rendered in muted neutral green (`#63756A`).

### Form Inputs & Controls
- **Inputs:** Pill or `8px` rounded fields with a quiet border (`1px solid rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.15)`), background in pure white, placeholder in `#8FA382`. Focus brings a crisp `#407B57` outline with zero harsh drop shadow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Curved organic corners (`4px` for checkbox, full circle for radio), filled with `#1B4D3E` on selection, with soft white glyphs.
